Robin PRO adds a **Robin PRO Gallery** section to your theme editor. Drop it
into any template, paste your gallery's ID, and the gallery shows up on your
storefront. No code, nothing to paste into a page's HTML.

## Before you start

Two things need to be in place:

- **The Robin PRO app embed is on.** The section only renders when the app
  embed is turned on in your current theme. If it's off, Robin PRO shows a
  banner with a **Turn on in theme editor** button. Click it, then click
  **Save** in the theme editor.
- **Your theme is an Online Store 2.0 theme.** All of Shopify's current free
  themes, like Dawn and Horizon, are. Older themes can't take app sections. If
  yours is one of them, use the
  [embed code](/docs/publish-galleries-with-embed-code) instead.

## Copy the gallery ID

Open your gallery in Robin PRO, go to the **Publish** tab and stay on the
**Theme** sub-tab. Click **Copy** next to the **Gallery ID**. You'll paste it
into the section in a moment.

## Add a gallery section to your theme

<Loom id="7b15ac867e4449e8a13fc928b77add54" />

1. In Robin PRO, click **Open theme editor** on the **Theme** sub-tab. Or, in
   your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Themes → Customize**.
2. Use the dropdown at the top of the editor to pick the template you want, for
   example **Home page** or **Products → Default product**.
3. In the sidebar, click **Add section**, open the **Apps** tab and choose
   **Robin PRO Gallery**.
4. Paste the gallery ID into the **Gallery ID** field.
5. Drag the section to where the gallery should go, and click **Save**.

Until you paste an ID, the theme editor shows a hint in place of the gallery.
Your visitors never see that hint. An empty section shows nothing on the live
storefront.

Want two galleries on the same template? Add two sections, each with its own
gallery ID.

## Add a gallery to just one page

This is the part that catches most people out.

Your Shopify pages don't each have their own layout. They all share the
**Default page** template. So if you open **Pages → Default page** in the theme
editor and add a gallery section there, the gallery appears on **every** page
that uses that template: your About page, your Contact page, your FAQ, all of
them.

To put a gallery on one page only, give that page its own template.

<Loom id="f4088be8447648e6911ae48886a3e4f9" />

### 1. Create a new page template

1. In the theme editor, open the dropdown at the top and go to **Pages**.
2. Click **Create template**.
3. Give it a name that says what it's for, like `gallery` or `wedding-photos`.
   Leave it based on **Default page** and click **Create template**.

The editor switches to your new template. It starts as a copy of the default
one, so it already has the page title and content.

### 2. Add the gallery section to it

Click **Add section**, open the **Apps** tab and choose **Robin PRO Gallery**.
Paste the gallery ID, drag the section where you want it, and click **Save**.

You're editing the new template only, so the rest of your pages stay as they
were.

### 3. Assign the template to your page

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Pages** and open the page.
2. Find the **Template** dropdown on the right and pick the template you just
   created.
3. Click **Save**.

Open the page on your storefront and the gallery is there. Every other page
still uses **Default page** and looks exactly as before.

Need a gallery on another page too? Create another template for it. Or, if a
few pages should show the same gallery, assign them all the same template.

## When a section isn't the right fit

A section sits between your theme's other sections. It can't go in the middle
of your page text. If you need that, like a gallery halfway through a blog
post or inside a product description, use the
[embed code](/docs/publish-galleries-with-embed-code).

And if the gallery just needs to sit above or below a page's content, you can
skip the theme editor entirely. See
[Publishing a gallery to a page](/docs/publish-a-gallery-to-a-page).

## Troubleshooting

**The gallery shows up on all your pages.** You added the section to the
**Default page** template. Remove it from there, then follow
[Add a gallery to just one page](#add-a-gallery-to-just-one-page).

**Your new template isn't in the Template dropdown.** Templates belong to a
theme. Make sure you created it in your **current** (live) theme, not in a draft
copy, and that you clicked **Save**.

**Robin PRO Gallery isn't under Apps.** Your theme may be too old for app
sections. Use the [embed code](/docs/publish-galleries-with-embed-code) with a
**Custom liquid** section instead.

**The section is there but no gallery appears.** Check that the Robin PRO app
embed is on. Robin PRO shows a banner when it's off. Also check that the
gallery ID is complete. Copy a fresh one from the **Theme** sub-tab if you're
not sure.

**You get an upgrade message instead of your photos.** The free plan covers up
to 12 images. Upgrading lifts the limit.
