The quickest way to get a gallery onto your storefront is to publish it to a
Shopify page directly from Robin PRO. You pick a page, choose whether the
gallery goes above or below the page's content, and the app does the rest. No
code, nothing to paste.

## Publish it

Open your gallery, go to the **Publish** tab and stay on the **Page** sub-tab.
You'll see a table of your store's pages:

| Column          | What it tells you                                                     |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Title           | The page's name in Shopify                                            |
| Page visibility | **Visible** if the page is live on your storefront, **Hidden** if not |
| Gallery         | **Added** if this gallery is already on that page                     |
| Location        | Where the gallery goes, **After** or **Before**                       |

Find the page you want, set **Location**, and click **Publish**. You'll get a
confirmation and the **Gallery** column flips to **Added**.

**After** puts the gallery below the page's existing content. **Before** puts it
above. **After** is the default, and it's usually what you want, since visitors
read your intro text first and then hit the photos.

Use the eye icon at the end of the row to open the page on your storefront and
check the result.

## Change your mind

To move a gallery from below the content to above it, just change **Location**
and hit **Publish** again. Robin PRO replaces the existing placement rather than
adding a second copy, so you can republish as often as you like.

To take the gallery off a page entirely, click **Remove** next to the **Added**
badge.

## Start from a new page

If you don't have a page for the gallery yet, click **New page**. Robin PRO
pre-fills the title with the gallery's name, which you can change, and creates
the page in Shopify for you. It then shows up in the table like any other, ready
to publish to.

Note that a brand new page starts out **Hidden**. Publish the page from your
Shopify admin when you're ready for visitors to see it.

## When this isn't enough

Publishing to a page covers a lot of ground, but it has two limits:

- It only works with Shopify **pages**, not products, collections, or blog posts.
- The gallery goes above or below your content, never in the middle of it.

When you need something else, like a gallery halfway through a blog post, under
a product description, or in a collection description, use the gallery's embed
code instead. See [Publishing galleries with embed code](/docs/publish-galleries-with-embed-code).

There's also a **Robin PRO Image Gallery** section in your theme editor if you'd
rather add galleries there.

## Troubleshooting

**You just published and the page looks unchanged.** Shopify sometimes serves a
cached copy of the page for a few seconds after an update. Wait a moment and
reload before assuming anything went wrong.

**The gallery isn't showing on the storefront.** Check the **Page visibility**
column. A **Hidden** page isn't live yet, no matter what's on it, so publish the
page itself in your Shopify admin.

**Nothing appears on a page that's Visible and marked Added.** Make sure Robin
PRO is enabled on your dashboard. Disabling the app hides every gallery on your
store, and Shopify can take up to a minute to catch up once you re-enable it.

**You get an upgrade message instead of your photos.** The free plan covers up
to 12 images. Upgrading lifts the limit.
