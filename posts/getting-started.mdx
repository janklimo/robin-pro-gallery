Welcome to Robin PRO! This short guide walks you through creating your first
photo gallery and embedding it on your Shopify store in just a few minutes.

## Install the app

Head to the [Robin PRO Image Gallery](https://apps.shopify.com/robin-pro-image-gallery)
listing on the Shopify App Store and click **Install**. Once installed, the app
appears in your Shopify admin under **Apps**.

## Create your first gallery

Open the app and click **New gallery**. Give it a name, then drag and drop your
images to upload them in bulk. You can reorder images, add captions, and set
alt text for accessibility — all without touching any code.

## Embed it on your store

Robin PRO integrates with the Shopify theme editor. Add the Robin PRO section to
any page, select the gallery you just created, and save. Your gallery is now
live, optimized, and lazy-loaded from a fast CDN.
